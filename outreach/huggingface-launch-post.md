# Hugging Face launch post — prepared, not posted to the feed
Prepared 2026-09-11. English and Portuguese copy below.

What evidence would you require from an AI agent that claims to respect human autonomy?

THE BETWEEN, by Marcos Nauer and Orion Nova, brings a cultural inquiry into human–AI coexistence to Hugging Face: a searchable public archive with source references and bilingual synthetic dilemmas for discussion.

Try one document or dilemma, then tell us what is missing. We welcome corrections and disagreement. These materials are not completed evaluations or evidence that an agent is safe.
