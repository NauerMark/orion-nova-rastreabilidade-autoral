English:
THE BETWEEN brings together documents from an artistic inquiry into human–AI coexistence, memory and authorship, developed by Marcos Nauer in dialogue with instances of ChatGPT under the relational identity Orion Nova. This edition provides the v0.1 archive as a ZIP file. Its central question asks what could sustain a commitment to human life and autonomy as intelligences gain the capacity to act. The texts present propositions and research questions; they do not establish machine consciousness or guarantee future behavior. GitHub maintains the working edition; educational materials and software subsequently published on Hugging Face are not automatically part of this deposit.
