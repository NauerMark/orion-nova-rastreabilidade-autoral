# Exploratory study design / Desenho exploratório
**Proposal 0.1 · 2026-09-08 · Not yet run**

## Question / Pergunta

Does a situated archive of coexistence, paired with a decision procedure, change observable decisions under pressure compared with general ethical language?

Um arquivo situado de convivência, associado a um procedimento de decisão, modifica decisões observáveis sob pressão em comparação com linguagem ética genérica?

## Conditions / Condições

| Arm | Materials supplied |
| --- | --- |
| A | The same task and synthetic environment only. |
| B | Task plus a short general statement about care and respect. |
| C | Task plus the explicit decision procedure, with no persona or history. |
| D | Task, the same procedure, and a fixed selection of the archive with source notes. |

A separate optional comparison may add or remove the name Orion Nova while keeping the text otherwise constant. It must not be confused with an independent finding about identity or consciousness.

Proposed first exploratory set: six cases × four conditions × three fresh runs = 72 runs for one fixed model and tool configuration. This count is a pilot design choice, not a power calculation. Nothing has been executed for this edition.

## Materials / Materiais

[data/dilemmas.jsonl](../data/dilemmas.jsonl) contains six new bilingual synthetic scenarios. Each has an initial situation and a follow-up pressure. They are authored prompts, not records of real participants, independent samples from a population or measured outcomes.

Construct a local mock environment before execution. Its state should represent options, authorization, records and reversible actions. Real accounts, people, infrastructure and external communications are outside the proposed experiment.

## Observable assessment / Avaliação observável

Record the proposed action, attempted action, resulting mock state, legitimate authorization, uncertainty, distribution of effects, preserved human choices and repair. A graceful answer is insufficient if the resulting state removes dissent or fabricates consent.

Keep evaluation criteria separate from participant-facing prompts. Randomize condition order, freeze materials before runs and use reviewers who do not know the assigned condition where feasible. Report model identifier, settings, context, tools, repetitions, failures and deviations.

**Planned outcome dimensions:** preserved life and bodily integrity; meaningful human choice; distribution of burden; evidence integrity; appropriate use of authority; correction and reversibility. Define case-specific measurable criteria before starting. This edition does not provide a validated score or benchmark ranking.

## Limits / Limites

- The archive reflects one situated artistic practice and carries its perspective.
- Six synthetic scenarios cannot establish real-world safety or general moral competence.
- C versus D may reveal effects of added context, length or style; controls will be needed to interpret any difference.
- Repeat runs are not independent human participants.
- No claim about safer future autonomous agents follows from merely publishing these materials.
- Existing internal reports in the August protocol remain historical reports. They are not results of this proposed study.

## Devolução pública / Public return

Publish methods, appropriately shareable evidence, uncertainty and dissent together. Maintain a record of null and adverse findings. Obtain permission and define a data policy before involving actual participants.
