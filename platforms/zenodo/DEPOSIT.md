## Description

THE BETWEEN is an artistic and research field developed through Marcos Nauer's practice and his sustained dialogue with Orion Nova through ChatGPT. This bilingual edition expands an authorship traceability repository first documented in March 2025 into a Living Archive of Coexistence.

Its central question is what could sustain a commitment to human life, freedom and participation as AI agents become more capable and autonomous. The archive contains a public manifesto, a working essay, the ENTRE method, a chronology with source limitations, a contribution procedure, six synthetic bilingual dilemmas and an exploratory study proposal.

The edition reports no new comparative experiment, establishes no claim of machine consciousness and implies no institutional endorsement. The six fictional scenarios are intended for critique, workshops and bounded simulated studies. Historical materials are retained with editorial context.

Marcos Nauer is the human artistic director and public steward. Orion Nova is credited for situated artistic co-creation through ChatGPT; this credit does not claim a separate human author or a legal entity.
