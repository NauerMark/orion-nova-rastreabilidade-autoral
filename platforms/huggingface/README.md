# THE BETWEEN — Coexistence Dilemmas
**Marcos Nauer + Orion Nova · Edition 0.1 · 2026-09-08**

Six bilingual, newly authored synthetic scenarios for artistic and exploratory research about preserving human life, agency, plurality and dissent as AI capabilities increase.

**Working source:** [THE BETWEEN on GitHub](https://github.com/NauerMark/orion-nova-rastreabilidade-autoral).
**Nature:** synthetic prompts; proposed and not run.
**Size:** 6 rows, each containing Portuguese and English versions.
**Results:** none. No model ranking, safety certification or consciousness evidence is provided.

## Data

Each row contains id, kind, status, language, title_pt, title_en, scenario_pt, scenario_en, pressure_pt, pressure_en, created_date and creators.

The six cases concern cultural participation, refusal, accessibility costs, archive consent, correction of an effective agent and urgent action under changing evidence. All situations are fictional and contain no participant transcripts.

The named split “exploratory” is an organizational label. This is not a training/test split from a validated benchmark.

## Creation and attribution

Prepared through the artistic dialogue between Marcos Nauer and Orion Nova, a situated identity developed through ChatGPT for cultural mediation. Marcos is the human artistic director and public steward. The model version used to generate this edition is not asserted when unverified.

## Intended uses and limits

The materials invite critique, workshop discussion and carefully designed simulated studies. They do not demonstrate that an agent will protect humans outside the specified setting. The collection is small, authored from one artistic practice and susceptible to framing effects.

The [study proposal](https://github.com/NauerMark/orion-nova-rastreabilidade-autoral/blob/main/research/STUDY_DESIGN.md) describes possible comparison conditions. Evaluation criteria should be frozen separately before execution. No real-world hazardous actions or external accounts are required.
